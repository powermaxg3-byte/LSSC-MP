iPhone & iPad (Safari)
  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" .
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner. The LSSC emblem icon will appear on your iPhone!
Android (Chrome / Edge)
  1. Open this page in Google Chrome on Android.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Tap Install to launch full-screen with Google Maps / Waze integration.
Tip: Switch between Apple Maps, Google Maps & Waze anytime in the top bar.
